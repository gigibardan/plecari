Moldova doesn't appear on any top European destination lists. It has no Eiffel Towers, no medieval squares crowded with Japanese tourists and selfie sticks, no Michelin guides on every street corner.

What Moldova has is wine. <cite index="399-1">Vineyards cover 7% of the country's territory — one of the highest vine densities per capita in the world</cite> — and a viticultural tradition proven over 3,500 years (cultivated grape seeds dating to 2800 BC have been found at Vărvăreuca). <cite index="406-1">Today, 90% of Moldovan wine is exported to 75 countries, with 60% of production going to European Union countries.</cite>

Below the earth around the capital stretches Europe's unique wine tourism infrastructure: two underground cellars of astronomical scale, connected by a total of 320 km of galleries carved into limestone, with millions of bottles stored at constant temperature. Cricova and Mileștii Mici aren't just wineries — they are geological and industrial spectacles with no equivalent anywhere in the world.

But beyond the underground colossi, Moldova offers something equally valuable: surface wineries with elegant architecture, authentic wines from grape varieties virtually impossible to find anywhere else on the planet, and a value-for-money ratio that makes any sommelier from Bordeaux raise an eyebrow.

## Why Moldova for Wine Tourism — Global Context

### World Records

<cite index="393-1">Mileștii Mici holds the Guinness World Record for the world's largest wine collection: 2 million bottles in galleries 200 km long, descending to 85 metres underground.</cite>

Cricova is second, with 120 km of galleries forming a real underground city with <cite index="393-1">streets, traffic lights and road signs — toured by electric vehicle.</cite>

### International Awards

<cite index="399-1">Château Purcari is the most awarded producer in Central and Eastern Europe at Decanter London.</cite> Negru de Purcari won a gold medal in Paris in 1878 and entered the Decanter Top 100 Classic Wines.

### Unique Prices

A quality wine bottle at the winery shop: 3–15 EUR. A glass at a restaurant: 1–3 EUR. Wines comparable to 40–80 EUR Bordeaux or Barossa, at 8–15 EUR. This is not about inferior quality — it's about extremely low local production costs.

## Moldova's 3 Wine Regions

Moldova has 4 Protected Geographical Indications (PGI), recognised in both Moldova and the EU:

### Codru — The Central Heart

<cite index="408-1">Codru is in central Moldova, on wooded hills around Chișinău.</cite> The closest region to the capital and the most diverse.

**Characteristics:** temperate climate, rich loessial soil, moderate altitudes (150–300 m). Produces fresh white wines and light-medium reds.

**Typical wines:** Feteasca Albă, Feteasca Regală, Sauvignon Blanc for whites; lighter Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon for reds.

**Main wineries:** Cricova, Mileștii Mici, Château Vartely, Castel Mimi, Asconi.

### Ștefan Vodă — The Southeast with Maritime Influence

<cite index="408-1">Ștefan Vodă is in south-eastern Moldova, on river terraces of the Dniester and Botna, with the moderating influence of the Black Sea.</cite>

**Characteristics:** <cite index="408-1">310–320 sunny days per year, iron-rich clay and loam soils, 57% red varieties.</cite> This is the home of Moldova's great red wines.

**Typical wines:** Feteasca Neagră, Rară Neagră, full-bodied Cabernet Sauvignon, complex red blends.

**Main wineries:** Château Purcari (founded 1827), Et Cetera.

**Why it's special:** <cite index="399-1">Negru de Purcari, Moldova's flagship wine, comes from this region — a blend of Cabernet Sauvignon, Georgian Saperavi and Moldovan Rară Neagră.</cite>

### Valul lui Traian — The Warm South

<cite index="408-1">Valul lui Traian is Moldova's southernmost and warmest region, close to the Danube plain.</cite>

**Characteristics:** higher temperatures, longer summers, fuller-bodied wines with more alcohol.

**Typical wines:** rich red blends, dessert wines, some aromatic whites.

## Moldova's Native Grape Varieties — The Planet's Unique Finds

This is the information many wine lovers don't know: Moldova has grape varieties virtually impossible to find anywhere else in the world. When you drink an authentic Feteasca Neagră or a Rară Neagră, you're tasting something unique.

### Feteasca Neagră (Black Maiden Grape)

<cite index="396-1">Feteasca Neagră is Moldova's most cultivated and most awarded native red variety</cite> — a grape with strong personality, with notes of blackberries, black cherries, plums and well-integrated tannin. Some sommeliers compare it to Australian Shiraz or Italian Sagrantino.

**Styles:** from light, fruity wines to large, concentrated versions with 10–15 year ageing potential.

**Where to find it:** at virtually any winery in Moldova.

### Rară Neagră

A rarer and more delicate variety than Feteasca Neagră — <cite index="396-1">medium-bodied red with soft tannins and a distinctive floral-fruity aroma.</cite> Often used in top blends (including Negru de Purcari).

### Feteasca Albă (White Maiden Grape)

<cite index="396-1">Fresh, lively white with notes of green apples and citrus</cite> — Moldova's most characterful white wine. Easy-drinking, versatile with food.

### Viorica

Perhaps Moldova's most exotic variety — an aromatic white with <cite index="399-1">intense floral perfume, unique in the world, produced in limited quantities.</cite> If you find it at a winery, buy it.

## Main Wineries — Complete Profile

### 1. Mileștii Mici — The World's Largest Wine Cellar

**Distance from Chișinău:** 18 km south-west (~25 minutes by car)
**Galleries:** <cite index="393-1">200 km, maximum depth 85 m, 2 million bottles — Guinness World Record</cite>
**Founded:** 1969 (galleries existed from 1950 as limestone mines)
**Visit:** electric vehicles through galleries
**Standard tour + tasting price:** ~35–50 EUR per person
**Winery shop:** wines from 3–8 EUR per bottle — unique prices in Europe
**Speciality:** rare dessert wines, vintage collector's wines
**Booking:** required at milestii-mici.md

**What makes it different from Cricova:** Mileștii Mici impresses through raw scale — the atmosphere is less theatrical, more industrially authentic. Shop prices are even lower than Cricova.

### 2. Cricova — The Underground City of Wine

**Distance from Chișinău:** 14 km north (~20 minutes)
**Galleries:** <cite index="393-1">120 km, most elaborate underground museum — with cinema, themed exhibitions, banquet halls</cite>
**Founded:** 1952
**Visit:** electric vehicles, 2–3 hours
**Tour without tasting price:** from 20 EUR
**Tour with tasting** (4 wines + sparkling): from 40 EUR
**Speciality:** sparkling wines produced by the classic Champagne method — among the best in Eastern Europe
**Booking:** required at cricova.md
**VIP fact:** Vladimir Putin, Angela Merkel and John Kerry have stored personal collections at Cricova

### 3. Château Purcari — Moldova's Most Awarded Wine

**Distance from Chișinău:** 70 km south-east (~1 hour)
**Founded:** <cite index="399-1">1827 — Moldova's oldest active commercial producer</cite>
**Estate:** 800+ hectares of vineyard in the Ștefan Vodă region
**Tour + tasting price:** 40–60 EUR per person (includes Moldovan lunch)
**Flagship wine:** <cite index="399-1">Negru de Purcari — Cabernet Sauvignon + Georgian Saperavi + Rară Neagră, in Decanter Top 100 Classic Wines</cite>
**Unique speciality:** <cite index="401-1">Freedom Blend — Saperavi + Rară Neagră + Bastardo, created in 2014 as a manifesto for the freedom of Ukraine, Moldova and Georgia. Profits go to Ukrainian refugees.</cite>
**Accommodation:** boutique hotel at the winery (from 80–150 EUR per night)
**Restaurant:** fine dining inspired by Moldovan and European cuisine

### 4. Castel Mimi — The Complete Wine Resort Experience

**Distance from Chișinău:** ~55 km (~45 minutes)
**Location:** Bulboaca, Anenii Noi district
**Founded:** <cite index="399-1">1893, by Constantin Mimi — the last governor of Bessarabia</cite>
**Architecture:** elegantly restored château, Moldova's most beautiful winery setting
**Tour + tasting price:** 30–50 EUR per person
**Accommodation:** boutique hotel, spa, pool (from 100–200 EUR per night)
**Restaurant:** one of Moldova's best rural restaurants
**Speciality:** elegant white wines from the Codru region, wine & food pairing experiences

### 5. Château Vartely — Accessible, Modern, Versatile

**Distance from Chișinău:** 20 km north (~20 minutes — closest surface winery)
**Founded:** 2003
**Tour + tasting price:** 15–30 EUR per person
**Accommodation:** villas overlooking the vineyard (from 60–120 EUR per night)
**Restaurant:** fine dining with terrace
**Speciality:** aromatic whites, some premium reds
**Walk-in possible:** yes, in season — but booking recommended

### 6. Et Cetera — Premium Boutique from the South

**Distance from Chișinău:** ~80 km (~1 hour)
**Family-owned:** focus on quality and personalised hospitality
**Tour + tasting price:** 25–40 EUR per person
**Speciality:** premium Feteasca Neagră, innovative blends
**Why it's worth it:** genuine personal experience — the owners welcome you themselves.

### 7. Asconi Winery — Rustic and Authentic

**Distance from Chișinău:** ~30 km
**Focus:** traditional hospitality, excellent Moldovan food, rustic cottages
**Tour + tasting price:** 20–35 EUR per person
**Speciality:** Feteasca Neagră, authentic house wines
**Accommodation:** vineyard cottages (from 40–70 EUR per night) — the cheapest winery stay in Moldova

## Moldova's Wine Festivals

### National Wine Day (first weekend of October)

<cite index="399-1">National Wine Day is the largest wine festival in Eastern Europe, held on the Chișinău esplanade for 25 consecutive years.</cite> Dozens of wineries participate with stands, tastings are free or token-priced (5–20 MDL per glass), with live music and Moldovan cuisine.

**2026:** 3–4 October. Book accommodation 2–3 months ahead.

### Gustart Festival (August)

Ethnomusicological festival at Orheiul Vechi with traditional music, crafts and tastings of wine and local products. Unique atmosphere — medieval village + wine + folk music.

## Practical Tips for Wine Tourism in Moldova

**Booking is essential** at Cricova and Mileștii Mici — spots sell out, especially at weekends and during harvest season. Book at least 1–2 weeks ahead.

**Don't drive after the tour** — tastings involve 4–6 glasses. Plan a return taxi or a tour with transport included. Yandex Go from Chișinău: 100–200 MDL (5–10 EUR) for a ride to nearby wineries.

**Winery shop purchases** — take advantage. Prices at the Mileștii Mici shop (3–8 EUR/bottle) or Château Purcari (8–25 EUR/premium bottle) are unmatched anywhere. Take a few bottles home — hold baggage allowance permits.

**Autumn is the golden moment** — September–October, new wine is fermenting, the winery atmosphere is electric, accommodation prices are lower than summer.

**Native varieties** — beyond Feteasca Neagră and Albă, ask at any winery to show you varieties like Viorica, Rară Neagră, Plavai or Bastardo. They are rare and unique.
