## Why Athens surprises everyone

Athens has a reputation problem. People treat it as a stopover on the way to the islands. That's a mistake. This is a city where you can touch 2,500-year-old marble in the morning, eat a €3 souvlaki for lunch, browse contemporary art in a converted gas works in the afternoon, and drink cocktails on a rooftop with the illuminated Acropolis floating above you at night.

And it's affordable. A full day in Athens — transport, food, attractions, evening drinks — costs €50-75. Try that in Rome or Paris.

## Acropolis & Ancient Athens

### The Acropolis

The Parthenon, the Erechtheion, the Temple of Athena Nike — all on a rocky hill that's been sacred for 5,000 years. **Standard ticket: €20.** **Combined ticket: €30** (includes Acropolis + Ancient Agora + Roman Agora + Temple of Zeus + Hadrian's Library + Kerameikos + Aristotle's Lyceum — valid 5 days). The combined ticket is extraordinary value.

**Timing:** gates open at 8:00 AM. Be there at 7:45. By 10:00, tour groups arrive and it's crowded. By noon in summer, the heat on the exposed rock is punishing (40°C+, zero shade).

**[Skip-the-line guided tours](https://www.getyourguide.com/athens-l128/?partner_id=EKAA80H)** (€30-50) include the history that makes the stones come alive — without a guide, it's just impressive ruins. With a guide, it's the birthplace of democracy, theatre, and philosophy.

### Acropolis Museum

At the foot of the Acropolis. Modern glass building with the original Caryatids, friezes, and artifacts. €15. One of the best archaeological museums in Europe. The top floor has a glass floor showing excavations beneath.

### Ancient Agora

The marketplace where Socrates debated and democracy was practised. Much less crowded than the Acropolis, equally atmospheric. Included in the combined ticket.

### Temple of Olympian Zeus

15 massive Corinthian columns (out of the original 104). Free view from outside the fence; combined ticket for inside.

## The neighbourhoods — where Athens really lives

### Plaka — the old village

Cobblestone streets beneath the Acropolis, neoclassical houses, tavernas with live music. Touristy but genuinely charming. Walk through in the evening for the atmosphere.

### Monastiraki — flea market and energy

Sunday flea market is legendary — everything from antique furniture to €2 vinyl records. Monastiraki Square has the best gyro stands in Athens (Thanasis, Savvas — €3-4 for a wrap that's a full meal).

### Psyrrí — food and nightlife

The neighbourhood between Monastiraki and Omonia has Athens' best concentration of restaurants, cocktail bars, and live music venues. Dinner here is excellent and affordable (€10-15 for a full meal with wine).

### Exarcheia — the alternative quarter

Street art, independent bookshops, anarchist history, and the most authentic Athens atmosphere. Not for everyone — it's gritty — but it's real. The bars and restaurants are the cheapest in central Athens.

### Kolonaki — upscale and café culture

Athens' poshest neighbourhood, on the slopes of Lycabettus Hill. Designer shops, gallery openings, and people-watching at sidewalk cafés. Take the funicular up Lycabettus (€10 return) for the best 360° view of Athens.

## Where to eat

**Gyro/souvlaki** — Athens' street food is legendary. A pita wrap with meat, tzatziki, tomato, onion, and chips: €3-4. Eat it standing up — it's better that way. **O Thanasis** (Monastiraki) and **Kosta Souvlaki** (Mitropoleos) are the classics.

**Taverna meal** — Greek salad + grilled meat or fish + wine: €10-15/person. **Klimataria** (live rembetiko music), **Oineas** (modern Greek, Psyrrí), **To Kafeneio** (Plaka, traditional).

**Seafood** — head to Piraeus harbour (30 minutes by metro) for the freshest fish in Attica. **Varoulko Seaside** for fine dining (€30+), or the small tavernas along Mikrolimano for honest grilled fish (€12-18).

**Market food** — Varvakios Central Market is Athens' belly: fresh fruit, cheese, olives, spices. Buy provisions for a picnic on Filopappou Hill.

## Day trips from Athens

### Hydra (1 hour by ferry, €30-60 return)

No cars, no motorbikes — donkeys and walking only. An artists' island with stone mansions, a perfect harbour, and crystal swimming spots. The most photogenic day trip from Athens.

### Aegina (40 minutes by ferry, €15-25 return)

Temple of Aphaia (one of Greece's best-preserved ancient temples), pistachio groves (Aegina pistachios are famous), and a pleasant harbour town. More accessible and cheaper than Hydra.

### Cape Sounion (1.5 hours by bus, €7)

Temple of Poseidon perched on a cliff 60m above the Aegean. The sunset here is one of the most famous in Greece. [KTEL bus from Athens](https://www.getyourguide.com/athens-l128/cape-sounion-tours-tc24/?partner_id=EKAA80H) or [rent a car](https://discovercars.tpo.mx/rR73qao3) for flexibility.

## Getting around

**Metro:** 3 lines, covers Acropolis, Monastiraki, Syntagma, Piraeus (for ferries). Single ticket: €1.20. 24h pass: €4.10. The stations at Syntagma and Acropolis double as archaeological museums (finds displayed behind glass).

**Walking:** central Athens is compact. Acropolis to Monastiraki: 10 minutes. Monastiraki to Syntagma: 5 minutes.

**[Bolt/Uber](https://airalo.tpo.mx/mYekFAo3):** works well in Athens. Much safer than street taxis (which have a reputation for rigged meters). [Scam guide](/en/asigurari/travel-scams-europe-2026).

**Airport to centre:** metro Line 3 (€9, 40 minutes to Syntagma). Bus X95 (€5.50, 60-70 minutes). Taxi: €40 (fixed rate, daytime).

## Practical tips

**Sunset spot:** Filopappou Hill (free, 15-minute walk from Acropolis metro). Better than crowded Lycabettus and you get the Acropolis in your sunset photo.

**Sunday:** many museums are free the first Sunday of the month (November-March).

**Water:** Athens tap water is safe and good quality. Carry a refillable bottle.

**Heat:** June-August can hit 40°C+. Plan outdoor sightseeing before 11 AM and after 5 PM. Siesta (2-5 PM) in your hotel or a shaded café is the Greek way. Read our [climate guide](/en/city-breaks/overtourism-europe-2026-tourist-taxes) for best timing.
