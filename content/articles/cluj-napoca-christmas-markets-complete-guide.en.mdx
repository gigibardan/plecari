Cluj-Napoca has one advantage over every other large Romanian city in December: time. While most markets pack up on 26 December or in the first days of January, Unirii Square stays lit for 43 days, and the Winter Market across town keeps its ice rink open into mid-February. Miss Cluj in December and you still have six weeks to fix the mistake.

The 2026 edition is called **„Planeta Crăciun”** — Christmas Planet — it opens on **Friday 20 November 2026**, and it is produced by the team behind UNTOLD, one of Europe's largest electronic music festivals. That shows immediately in the production values: a redesigned stage, rebuilt entrance gates, and a light show projected onto the dome in the middle of the square, synchronised to a soundtrack written for the occasion.

This guide covers everything you need to plan a visit: official dates, opening hours, real prices from previous editions, how to get there from across Europe, where to stay, where to eat, and what to see between two cups of mulled wine.

## What is new in 2026

**The dome light show.** Projections animate the dome in the middle of Unirii Square in an immersive format, synchronised with a soundtrack composed for this edition. Each performance lasts ten minutes and runs four times each evening: **17:00–17:10, 19:00–19:10, 20:00–20:10 and 21:30–21:40**. The 17:00 slot is new — in 2025 the first was at 19:00. For families with young children it is the single most useful change: you can catch the show, a Ferris wheel ride and a hot chocolate and still be back at the hotel by bedtime.

**A redesigned square.** New stage design, rebuilt access gates and a reworked layout compared with 2025.

**The line-up.** Announced artists include **Smiley, Grasu XXL, Fuego, The Urs, Nouă Unșpe (911), Azur, Ilinca and Zodier**, alongside choirs, local bands and carol recitals. The full programme of 80-plus performances is released in stages through the season.

## The two Cluj markets

### 1. Planeta Crăciun — Unirii Square

This is the main market and the reason to make the trip. More than 85 wooden chalets selling crafts and food from local producers, the 20-metre tree at the centre, a Ferris wheel, a carousel, a **VR sleigh** that takes you virtually to Lapland, **Santa's House** with free creative workshops for children, a photo booth and skill-game stands with prizes.

The Santa's House workshops are announced for 2026 on a split schedule: **Monday to Thursday 18:00–20:00, Friday to Sunday 16:00–19:00**, with free access. Some sources list a wider 16:00–20:00 daily window — if the workshop is the main reason you are going, confirm on your first evening.

The whole square is pedestrianised and takes 20 to 30 minutes to walk at an easy pace, so you do not need a plan. St Michael's Church, the Gothic cathedral standing in the middle of the square, is the visual anchor of the market and ends up in almost every good photograph.

### 2. Winter Market — Horia Demian Sports Hall plateau

The ice rink is here, not in Unirii Square. This is such a common misunderstanding that it is worth repeating: since the 2023 contract, **the rink belongs to the Winter Market**, set up on Splaiul Independenței between the old town and the university campus, next to Central Park — about a 15-minute walk from Unirii Square.

In the Cluj County Council calendar, the Winter Market is listed as running until **15 February 2027**, meaning the rink operates long after the main market closes. The start date appears in different sources as either 27 November or 27 December 2026 and had not been confirmed by the organiser at the time of writing, so verify before building your plans around the rink.

Prices from the 2025–2026 season, as a benchmark:

| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| Rink access (one session) | 30 lei (~€6) |
| Skate hire | 20 lei (~€4) |
| Penguin / dolphin skating aid | 30 lei |
| Winter carousel | 15 lei |

Skaters enter in fixed sessions: **10:00–11:30, 12:00–13:30, 14:00–15:30, 16:00–17:30, 18:00–19:30 and 20:00–22:00**, with a 30-minute break between sessions to resurface the ice. The winter carousel ran daily from 11:00 to 22:00.

## What to eat

Cluj has a cuisine of its own, not just generic market food. Three things are worth hunting down specifically.

**Varza à la Cluj (Cluj-style cabbage).** A baked casserole of sauerkraut, pork, rice and sour cream. It is the dish that carries the city's name and appears on the menu of almost every traditional restaurant in the centre. At the market it turns up as street food served in flatbread, around 45 lei.

**Kürtőskalács.** The Transylvanian chimney cake, baked on a spit over embers and rolled in sugar, walnut, cinnamon, poppy seed or coconut. Cluj is one of the places in Romania where you will find it baked in front of you rather than reheated. For many locals, the smell of burnt caramel from the chimney cake stalls is the official smell of the season.

**Lángos.** Deep-fried dough with sour cream, cheese and garlic. Transylvania makes it better than the rest of the country, and it is the most calories-per-leu item at the entire market.

Beyond classic mulled wine, look for the white-wine versions and the alcohol-free options — hot apple cider and fruit teas have appeared at more and more stalls in recent years.

## From the airport to the centre

The airport is 9–12 km east of the centre on the DN1C road.

| Option | Duration | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **A1E** express bus → Mihai Viteazu Square | ~26 min | ~6 lei (€1.20) |
| Bus / trolleybus **lines 5 and 8** | ~26–35 min | ~3 lei (€0.60) |
| **5N** night bus | ~30 min | 5 lei (€1.00) |
| Metered taxi | 20–30 min | 30–50 lei (€6–10) |
| Bolt / Uber | 15–25 min | 25–45 lei (€5–9) |
| Pre-booked private transfer | 15–25 min | €15–25 |

From Mihai Viteazu Square it is a five-minute walk to Unirii Square — so with the A1E you go from aircraft to Christmas market in about half an hour for just over a euro.

**Check this:** the last bus leaves the airport around **22:50** on weekdays and **22:17** at weekends. After that you have the 5N, a taxi or the apps. If you land after midnight, pre-book a transfer. Our [airport transfer guide](/en/city-breaks/airport-transfer-guide) covers the general options.

Buy tickets from the machine at the stop, through the 24Pay app, or tap a contactless card on the validator on board many vehicles.

### Parking, if you drive

The surface car park in Unirii Square — nearly 100 spaces — was **closed and removed in September 2026** for archaeological work connected to the future metro station. The practical alternatives are the multi-storey car parks at Sala Polivalentă and Cluj Arena, a 15–20 minute walk through Central Park and rarely full, or the airport **Park & Ride**, where the parking ticket doubles as a public transport ticket on lines 5, 5N, 8, M41, M42, M43 and M44.

The historic centre is small and entirely walkable, so paying a premium for location is worth it: in December, the difference between "two minutes from the tree" and "twenty minutes in the cold" is real.

Well-known addresses in and near the centre: **Hotel Victoria**, **Capitolina City Chic Hotel**, **Opera Plaza**, **Grand Hotel Napoca**, **Hotel Beyfin** (Avram Iancu Square), **DoubleTree by Hilton Cluj – City Plaza**, **Courtyard by Marriott Cluj-Napoca Downtown**, **Hampton by Hilton**, the five-star **Hotel Platinia** with its spa, and boutique guesthouses such as **Guest House 1568**.

**When to book:** the weekends of 5–7, 12–14 and 19–21 December fill first, and New Year's Eve sells out from October. If your dates are flexible, a Tuesday-to-Thursday visit can cut accommodation costs by 30–40%.

## Where to eat

Cluj has one of Romania's strongest restaurant scenes, and unlike the market, prices here stay reasonable.

**On Unirii Square, steps from the tree:**
- **Nuka Bistro** (Piața Unirii 16) — Parisian-concept bistro with real character
- **Bujole** (Piața Unirii 15) — French-inspired cooking and specialty coffee
- **Toulouse Café Brasserie** (Piața Unirii 12) — one of the city's longest-running brasseries
- **Rhédey Café** (Piața Unirii 9) — good for lunch, right beside St Michael's

**For Transylvanian and Romanian cooking:** **Roata**, **Casa Boema**, **Livada**, **Baracca** (modern bistronomy, one of the best tables in the city) and **Samsara Foodhouse** for vegetarian.

**Cheap and good:** **Klausen Pubhouse** in Museum Square, **Gandhi** on Ferdinand 38 (weekday lunch menu around 21–30 lei), **Irish & Music Pub**, and **Cartofisserie** on Unirii Square.

Book ahead for dinner on December weekends. The good central restaurants fill up, and "we'll find somewhere after the market" usually ends in an hour's wait.

## What else to see in Cluj

You need a daytime plan, because the market only comes alive after dark.

**St Michael's Church** — the Gothic cathedral in the middle of Unirii Square, one of Romania's most important Gothic buildings, with the equestrian statue of Matthias Corvinus in front of it.

**The Pharmacy Museum** — in the building of the city's first pharmacy, on the corner of Unirii Square. Small, strange, and exactly the right kind of museum for a cold afternoon.

**Cetățuia Hill** — the hill above the city with the ruins of a Habsburg fortress and the best panorama of Cluj. A 20–25 minute climb, free to access. In winter, at sunset, with the city lighting up below, it is the best photograph you will take here.

**Museum Square** — the medieval piazza behind Unirii Square, full of cafés and terraces, with the nicest evening atmosphere in the city.

**Central Park and the Botanical Garden** — the Botanical Garden's heated tropical greenhouses turn into an unexpected advantage in December.

**The Romanian National Opera and the National Theatre** — in Avram Iancu Square. An opera performance on a December evening is an excellent way to fill the hours before the market.

For the full list of sights and the city outside the Christmas season, see our [complete Cluj-Napoca guide](/en/experiente/cluj-napoca-complete-guide).

## Winter day trips from Cluj-Napoca

**Turda Salt Mine (30 km, ~40 minutes).** The best cold-season trip in the region, for a simple reason: the underground chambers sit at a constant 12°C whatever the weather. The salt galleries hold a Ferris wheel, an underground lake with rowing boats, mini-golf and bowling, more than 100 metres below ground. Open daily **9:00–17:00** in 2026, with last underground entry at **16:20**. Tickets rose to 85 lei at weekends after the 2025 adjustment — check the current rate before you go.

**Turda Gorge (35 km).** Spectacular in winter too, but the path can be iced over. Proper boots and dry weather only.

**Bánffy Castle, Bonțida (30 km).** The "Versailles of Transylvania", under restoration and impressive even half-ruined. Check winter opening hours first.

**Alba Iulia (100 km, ~1h30).** The Alba Carolina citadel is one of the best-restored fortifications in Romania and runs its own holiday programme. See our [Alba Iulia guide](/en/experiente/alba-iulia-complete-guide).

**Sighișoara (160 km, ~2h30).** The inhabited medieval citadel, with snow on the roofs, looks exactly as you imagine it. See our [Sighișoara guide](/en/experiente/sighisoara-complete-guide).

**Oradea (155 km, ~2h15).** Romania's finest Art Nouveau ensemble, with a Christmas market of its own. Note there are **no direct trains from Cluj in 2026**, only replacement coaches. See our [Oradea guide](/en/experiente/oradea-complete-guide).

## Ready-made itineraries

### Short break (2 days, 1 night)

**Day 1.** Arrive in the afternoon, check in. 16:45 — walk into Unirii Square in the blue hour. 17:00 — first dome light show. A full circuit of the chalets, mulled wine, one turn on the Ferris wheel. 19:30 — booked dinner in the centre. 21:30 — the last projection and a drink in Museum Square.

**Day 2.** Morning: St Michael's Church and the Pharmacy Museum. Lunch on Universității street. Afternoon: climb Cetățuia for the panorama. Evening: one session at the Winter Market ice rink, then depart.

### Three days

Add a full day for **Turda Salt Mine** in the morning (leave at 9:00, back by 14:00), the Botanical Garden or Central Park in the afternoon, and a second pass through the market in the evening — this time with a shortlist of the chalets you liked.

### With children

The golden window is **16:00–19:00**: free workshops at Santa's House, the 17:00 light show, the carousel and the VR sleigh, then out before the crowds arrive. The ice rink at the Winter Market deserves a separate session, with a penguin skating aid for the little ones at 30 lei.

## Weather and what to wear

December in Cluj does not automatically mean snow. In recent years, December highs have frequently sat between **0 and 9°C**, with sub-zero nights and snowfall alternating with grey, damp days. Morning fog is common.

What matters, in order:

1. **Waterproof shoes with grip.** The paving in Unirii Square becomes slippery when melted snow refreezes.
2. **Layers**, not one thick coat. You will spend two to three hours standing outside, moving in and out of heated spaces.
3. **Thin touchscreen gloves.** You will take a lot of photographs.
4. **A small umbrella or a hood.** Cold rain is more likely than snow.

## Cluj compared with other Romanian markets

| City | Strength | Weakness | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cluj-Napoca** | Longest season (43 days, plus the rink into February), festival-grade production, a genuine food scene | Relatively small square, prices above the national average | A complete urban weekend, not just a market |
| **Sibiu** | The most authentic setting, medieval square, tradition | Gets extremely crowded, expensive accommodation | Classic postcard atmosphere |
| **Craiova** | The largest by area, spectacular installations | Less to do in the city itself | Families with children, pure spectacle |
| **Bucharest** | Easy to reach, several venues | Experience fragmented across squares | Anyone already in the capital |
| **Brașov** | Mountain setting, pairs with Poiana Brașov | Small market | Skiing plus a market in one weekend |

Cluj wins the "complete weekend" category. The market is a reason to come, not the whole holiday: there are museums, restaurants, a specialty coffee scene, an opera house, a fortress hill with a panorama, and a salt mine theme park 40 minutes away. Sibiu has the prettier square; Craiova has the bigger show; Cluj has a city around the market.

For comparison abroad, see our complete guide to the [Budapest Christmas markets](/en/calatorii/budapest-christmas-markets-complete-guide) and to [Craiova](/en/calatorii/craiova-christmas-markets-complete-guide), the largest in Romania.

## Practical tips — the mistakes everyone makes

**Do not look for the ice rink in Unirii Square.** It is at the Horia Demian Sports Hall, a 15-minute walk away. People lose whole evenings searching for it in the centre.

**Do not drive into the centre on a Saturday evening.** The Unirii Square car park no longer exists. Use the airport Park & Ride or the Polivalentă and Cluj Arena car parks.

**Carry some cash, but not much.** Most chalets take cards, though at peak times contactless moves faster than waiting for change. 100–200 lei covers anything.

**Check whether your mug carries a deposit.** If you do not want the souvenir, return it and take the money back.

**Go earlier than you think.** 17:00 on a weekday gives you the same lights as 20:00 on Saturday, without the crowd.

**Check the live stream before you set out.** The city hall camera above shows you, in real time, whether it is worth going now or waiting an hour.

**Do not build the whole trip around one concert.** The artistic programme is announced in stages and times can change. Treat concerts as a bonus.

---

## Is it worth it?

If you can only visit one Romanian Christmas market and you want a medieval postcard, choose Sibiu. If you want the biggest spectacle, choose Craiova.

If you want a **city** with a good market inside it — restaurants that matter, serious coffee, a fortress hill with a view, an opera house, a salt mine turned theme park 40 minutes away, and a square that stays lit for 43 days — Cluj is the answer. And it is one of the very few Romanian Christmas markets you can reach on a direct flight from London, Dublin, Milan or Munich.

Fly in on a Tuesday evening, stay until Thursday, and you will have Cluj almost to yourself.
