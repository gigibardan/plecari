## Why this is one of Europe's greatest drives

The Dalmatian Magistrala (road D8) runs 350 km between Split and [Dubrovnik](/en/city-breaks/dubrovnik-city-break-guide) — limestone cliffs plunging into turquoise water, red-roofed fishing villages, pine-scented air, and the Adriatic stretching to the horizon. You could drive it in 3.5 hours. But with 5 days and a [rental car](https://discovercars.tpo.mx/rR73qao3), you turn a simple transfer into one of Europe's most memorable holidays.

![Dalmatian coast — turquoise water and limestone cliffs](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1414862625453-d87604a607e4?w=900&q=80)

## Day 1: Split — Roman palace meets Mediterranean energy

**Driving:** 0 km (pick up car, explore)

Collect your [rental car](https://discovercars.tpo.mx/rR73qao3) at Split airport. Head straight to **Diocletian's Palace** — a 4th-century Roman emperor's retirement home that's now a living city. People live, eat, and shop INSIDE the ancient walls.

**Don't miss:** underground halls (€8, Game of Thrones filming location), cathedral bell tower (€7, best Split panorama), and the **Riva** waterfront promenade at sunset.

**Dinner:** peka (meat or octopus slow-cooked under iron bell) at Konoba Fetivi. Full meal: €15-20.

**Book [Split experiences on GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/split-l313/?partner_id=EKAA80H)** — walking tours, food tours, and boat trips to Blue Lagoon.

**Accommodation:** Split centre, from €60/night.

## Day 2: Split → Omiš → Brela → Makarska (90 km, 2h)

### Omiš (30 km) — pirate town + canyon rafting

Medieval pirate fortress at the mouth of the Cetina canyon. If you want adrenaline, [Cetina River rafting](https://www.getyourguide.com/omis-l2543/?partner_id=EKAA80H) (3h, €35-45) is one of Croatia's best outdoor experiences — Grade 2 rapids through a spectacular limestone canyon.

### Brela (60 km) — Croatia's most beautiful beach

Punta Rata beach: fine pebbles, ancient pine trees providing natural shade, and an iconic rock formation rising from turquoise water. Repeatedly voted Croatia's #1 beach. Stop for 2-3 hours. Free entry.

Featured in our [best European beaches guide](/en/cazari/best-beaches-europe-2026).

### Makarska (90 km) — mountains meet sea

The Makarska Riviera sits between the Adriatic and Biokovo mountains (1,762 m) — the backdrop is dramatic. Palm-lined promenade, harbour restaurants, views to Brač and Hvar islands.

**Accommodation:** Makarska or Brela, from €55/night.

## Day 3: Makarska → Biokovo → Pelješac Peninsula (120 km, 2.5h)

### Biokovo Skywalk

Glass viewing platform at **1,228 m altitude** — 60 km of coastline visible below. €6 + parking €8. Go early (before 10 AM) to avoid queues. The drive up the mountain road is an experience in itself.

### Pelješac Peninsula — Croatia's secret

The Pelješac Bridge (opened 2022) connects the peninsula to the mainland, bypassing the Bosnian Neum corridor entirely. Two unmissable stops:

**Ston** — the second-longest defensive wall in the world (after the Great Wall of China), 5.5 km across hills. Walk the walls (€12). Then descend for **fresh oysters** straight from the producer — 12 oysters + Plavac Mali wine: €15. Genuinely one of Europe's best food experiences.

**Dingač wine region** — Croatia's finest red. Cellar tastings: €10-15, 3-5 wines.

**Accommodation:** Ston or Orebić, from €50/night.

## Day 4: Pelješac → Trsteno → Dubrovnik (70 km, 1.5h)

### Trsteno Arboretum

Renaissance botanical garden (1502) with 500-year-old plane trees and the Neptune fountain — a [Game of Thrones](/en/city-breaks/dubrovnik-city-break-guide) filming location (King's Landing gardens). €7. Perfect 1-hour stop.

### Dubrovnik arrival

Park at Ilijaš garage (near cable car, €30/day — no cars in Old Town). Take the **cable car up Mount Srđ** (€27 return) for your first aerial view of the city at sunset.

**Accommodation:** Dubrovnik, 2 nights, from €70/night.

![Dubrovnik from Mount Srđ — terracotta rooftops and Adriatic](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555990793-da11153b2473?w=900&q=80)

## Day 5: Dubrovnik — walls, GoT, island

The full [Dubrovnik experience](/en/city-breaks/dubrovnik-city-break-guide):

**8:00** — City walls (€35). Go at opening — by 10:00 it's packed with cruise passengers. The full 1,940 m circuit: 1.5-2 hours above the sea.

**10:30** — [Game of Thrones walking tour](https://www.getyourguide.com/dubrovnik-l513/game-of-thrones-tours-tc358/?partner_id=EKAA80H) (2h, from €25). Real filming locations with scene comparisons.

**13:00** — Lunch at Pizzeria Tabasco (€7-12) or seafood at Konoba Dalmatino (€15-20).

**14:30** — Ferry to **Lokrum Island** (€15 return). Iron Throne replica, botanical garden, "Dead Sea" salt lake, crystal-clear swimming.

**18:00** — Buža Bar — cliff-edge platform outside the walls, beer with the best sunset view in the Adriatic.

## Car rental tips

**Full insurance (CDW + TP + Super CDW).** Croatian coastal roads have tight curves. A scratch costs €200-500 without cover. With Super CDW (€5-10/day extra): zero excess. [Compare on DiscoverCars](https://discovercars.tpo.mx/rR73qao3).

**Photograph the car at pickup.** Every scratch, all sides, including roof. Email photos to yourself as proof.

**Diesel or hybrid.** Fuel: €1.40-1.50/litre. 350 km in a small diesel: ~25 litres = ~€37.

**One-way fee:** €30-80. Worth it — saves a day of return driving.

**Don't park in Dubrovnik Old Town.** Pedestrianised. Ilijaš garage or Pile Gate parking.
