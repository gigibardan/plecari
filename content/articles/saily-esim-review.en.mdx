## What Saily is

Saily is a prepaid eSIM service that provides mobile data in **150+ countries**. The mechanics are identical to any eSIM provider:

1. Purchase a plan on saily.com or in the Saily app
2. Receive a QR code by email (instantly)
3. Scan the QR code in your phone's SIM settings
4. The eSIM profile installs in 30–60 seconds
5. At your destination: activate the eSIM profile and enable Data Roaming

Your primary SIM (physical) remains active throughout — you retain your regular phone number for calls and SMS while the eSIM handles data.

### What makes Saily different from other providers

**Built-in VPN.** The main differentiator versus Airalo and Yesim is the VPN layer inherited from NordVPN's technology. Saily automatically encrypts your connection on unsecured public Wi-Fi networks (hotels, cafés, airports). It also includes:

- **Ad blocker** — blocks web advertisements, reducing data consumption by up to 28.6% according to Saily's own measurements
- **Web protection** — automatically blocks access to known malicious domains (phishing, malware distribution)
- **115+ virtual locations** — you can appear to be browsing from another country

For travellers who regularly use hotel Wi-Fi, airport networks, or café hotspots, this is a meaningful differentiator — it adds a layer of security without requiring a separate VPN app or subscription.

**Most generous unlimited data threshold.** On unlimited plans, Saily offers **5 GB/day at full speed** before throttling to ~1 Mbps. This is the highest threshold on the market: Airalo offers 3 GB/day, and Nomad only 2 GB/day. For most travellers, 5 GB/day covers maps, social media, messaging, and even moderate video streaming.

## The VPN feature: what it actually does

"VPN included" can mean very different things. Here's what the Saily VPN does and doesn't do.

### What the Saily VPN does

- **Encrypts** your data connection on any network (including the eSIM data connection itself)
- **Protects** against packet sniffing on public Wi-Fi (someone on the same network intercepting your data)
- **Hides** your browsing activity from the local network administrator (the hotel, the café, the airport)
- **Blocks ads** on websites — reduces data consumption and improves browsing experience
- **Blocks malicious domains** — protection against phishing on unsecured networks
- **115+ virtual locations** — you can appear to be browsing from another country

### What the Saily VPN does NOT do

- **It's not the full NordVPN client** — it lacks advanced threat protection, dedicated IP addresses, obfuscated servers, and split tunnelling
- **It does not reliably unblock streaming services** (Netflix, Hulu, BBC iPlayer) the way a full VPN does
- **It does not bypass country-level internet restrictions** (Turkey's social media restrictions, China's Great Firewall)
- **It has not been independently audited** separately from NordVPN's main product

### Who it matters for

**It matters a lot if:** you regularly use hotel or airport Wi-Fi for banking, work email, or accessing sensitive data. The encryption provided is real protection against man-in-the-middle attacks.

**It doesn't matter if:** you already have a NordVPN/ExpressVPN/Surfshark subscription and use it on your phone anyway. You don't need Saily's VPN if you're already running one.

**Calculated value:** if Saily's VPN saves you from a separate VPN subscription (~€3–5/month), you save €36–60/year. On an eSIM plan of $10–15 per trip, that effectively makes Saily free over 3–4 trips.

## How to buy and install Saily

### Step 1: Check eSIM compatibility

Your phone must support eSIM:

**iPhone:** XS or newer (2018+). Check: Settings → General → About → look for "EID" = eSIM supported. Or dial *#06# — if EID appears, you're good.

**Samsung Galaxy:** S20 and newer, Z Fold/Z Flip all versions, A54 and newer (some A models).

**Google Pixel:** 3 and newer.

**Other Android:** check Settings → Connections → SIM card manager — if "Add eSIM" appears, your phone supports it.

**Note:** phones purchased through carriers may have eSIM locked. If you don't see the option, contact your carrier for unlocking.

### Step 2: Purchase your plan

1. Go to **saily.com** or download the Saily app (iOS / Android)
2. Select your destination (or regional/global plan)
3. Choose your data amount and duration
4. Pay by card — you receive the QR code by email **instantly**

### Step 3: Install at home, on Wi-Fi (critical)

Install **before departure** — not at the airport, not after landing. Stable home Wi-Fi is the correct environment.

**On iPhone:**
1. Settings → Mobile Data → Add eSIM
2. Tap "Use QR Code"
3. Scan the QR from the email
4. Label it (e.g. "Saily Travel")
5. Keep it as secondary / inactive until needed

**On Android (Samsung):**
1. Settings → Connections → SIM card manager → Add eSIM
2. Scan QR code
3. Confirm installation
4. Keep inactive until arrival

### Step 4: Activate at your destination

When you land:
1. Go to SIM settings
2. Select the Saily eSIM profile
3. Set it as your data SIM
4. Enable Data Roaming on the Saily profile
5. Internet starts within 30–60 seconds

### Step 5: Keep your primary SIM active

Your physical SIM stays active for calls, SMS, and bank verification codes. Set: Data → Saily, Calls and SMS → your physical SIM. Both work simultaneously.

Full installation guide: [how to install an eSIM step by step](/en/esim-roaming/how-to-install-esim).

### Installation tips

- **Screenshot the QR code** — if you lose the email, you have a backup
- **Install 24 hours before departure** — gives you time to troubleshoot any issues
- **Don't delete the eSIM profile** after data runs out — you can buy a new plan on the same profile
- **If it doesn't connect on landing:** restart your phone, or toggle the eSIM profile off and back on

## The Turkey exception: buy before you land

If your trip includes Turkey — and this applies equally to Saily, Airalo, and Yesim — you must purchase and install your eSIM **before** arriving.

Since 2025, Turkey's telecommunications authority (BTK) has blocked access to major international eSIM provider websites from within Turkey. If you try to buy after landing in Istanbul or Antalya, saily.com, airalo.com, and yesim.app simply **will not load** — not on Turkish mobile networks, not on local Wi-Fi.

The eSIM profile installed at home activates normally once you land — the block only affects the purchase/installation process, not the service itself. Once installed, it works perfectly.

**If you forgot to buy before departure and you're already in Turkey:**
- Use a VPN (if you have one) to access the provider's website
- Ask someone back home to purchase the plan and send you the QR code via WhatsApp/email
- Buy a local physical SIM at the airport (Turkcell, Vodafone TR, Türk Telekom) — ~€15–20 for 10 GB

For the complete guide to data options in Turkey and other non-EU destinations: [best eSIM by destination 2026](/en/esim-roaming/cel-mai-bun-esim-per-destinatie).

## 10 common eSIM mistakes to avoid

### 1. Buying after landing

Many provider websites don't load without Wi-Fi, and in Turkey they're blocked entirely. Buy and install **at home**.

### 2. Not enabling Data Roaming

The most common reason for "my eSIM isn't working": you forgot to enable Data Roaming on the eSIM profile. It's a separate toggle in your settings.

### 3. Deleting the eSIM profile

If you delete the profile, you need to buy a completely new plan. Deactivate it, don't delete it — you can reuse the same profile with a new plan later.

### 4. Forgetting to disable data on the physical SIM

If both SIMs have data enabled, your phone may use the physical SIM (with expensive roaming) instead of the eSIM. Explicitly set the eSIM as your data SIM.

### 5. Buying more data than you need

5 GB is enough for a week of moderate use. Don't buy 20 GB "just in case" — it's money wasted. You can purchase an additional plan at any time if you run out.

### 6. Ignoring the regional vs per-country difference

A Turkey 5 GB / 30-day plan costs ~$10. A Europe plan (which includes Turkey) for 5 GB / 30 days costs ~$15. If you're visiting only Turkey, choose the country-specific plan.

### 7. Not using free Wi-Fi

Hotel, restaurant, and airport Wi-Fi is free and doesn't consume your eSIM data. With Saily's VPN enabled, it's also secure. Use Wi-Fi for streaming and downloads; use eSIM data for navigation and messaging on the go.

### 8. Buying from unknown providers

The eSIM market has some questionable providers. Stick to the verified ones: Saily (NordVPN), Airalo (largest provider), Yesim (established). Don't buy from unknown websites found through Google ads.

### 9. Ignoring speed

Price isn't everything — an eSIM that costs $5 but runs at 5 Mbps is frustrating. Saily delivers 50–95 Mbps, Airalo 30–70 Mbps, Yesim 18–44 Mbps. If you need stable video calls, speed matters.

### 10. Not testing before departure

After installation, activate the eSIM profile for a few minutes and verify it connects (it should connect to a partner network in your home country or nearby area). Then deactivate and reactivate at your destination.

## The verdict: which eSIM is right for you

There is no universal winner — it depends on how you travel.

**Choose Saily if:** you want free VPN protection included, unlimited data with the most generous threshold (5 GB/day), a trusted brand (NordVPN), and you're primarily travelling to Europe or popular destinations. Best feature-to-price ratio on the market.

**Choose Airalo if:** you want the widest variety of plans, you're heading to a less common destination (200+ countries), and you prefer the lowest price on fixed-data plans.

**Choose Yesim if:** you need a virtual phone number integrated with your eSIM, and you're travelling within Europe long-term.

**The universal rule:** compare prices for your specific destination across all three websites before purchasing — it takes 5 minutes and saves $2–5 per trip.

What **doesn't** change regardless of provider: buy the eSIM **before departure**, install on home Wi-Fi, enable Data Roaming when you land, and keep your regular SIM active for calls and bank verification codes.
