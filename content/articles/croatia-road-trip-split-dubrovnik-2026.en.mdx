## Why this is one of Europe's greatest drives

The Dalmatian Magistrala (road D8) is 350 km of limestone cliffs, turquoise coves, red-roofed fishing villages, and the Adriatic stretching to the horizon. It's not the fastest route from Split to [Dubrovnik](/en/city-breaks/dubrovnik-city-break-guide) — but speed isn't the point. With 5 days and a [rental car](https://discovercars.tpo.mx/rR73qao3), you transform a 3.5-hour drive into one of the most memorable holidays you can have in Europe.

Croatia uses the euro (since January 2023), so no currency confusion. Card payments are accepted everywhere.

## Day 1: Split — Roman palace, waterfront, food

**Driving:** 0 km (pick up car, explore the city)

Pick up your [rental car](https://discovercars.tpo.mx/rR73qao3) at Split airport. First stop: **Diocletian's Palace** — not a museum behind ropes, but an actual living city inside a 4th-century Roman emperor's retirement home. People live, eat, and shop INSIDE the palace walls.

Walk through the underground halls (€8, [Game of Thrones filming location](/en/city-breaks/dubrovnik-city-break-guide)), climb the cathedral bell tower (best Split views, €7), and lose yourself in the narrow streets. Evening: the **Riva** waterfront promenade — where locals spend hours over a single coffee.

**Dinner:** peka (meat or octopus slow-cooked under an iron bell) at Konoba Fetivi. Full meal: €15-20.

**Accommodation:** Split centre or Bačvice (urban beach, 10-minute walk). From €60/night.

Book [Split experiences on GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/split-l313/?partner_id=EKAA80H) — walking tours, food tours, and Game of Thrones tours are available.

### Day 2: Split → Omiš → Brela → Makarska (90 km)

**Driving:** 2 hours with stops

**Omiš (30 km)** — medieval pirate town at the mouth of the Cetina canyon. If you want adrenaline, [Cetina River rafting](https://www.getyourguide.com/omis-l2543/?partner_id=EKAA80H) (3 hours, €35-45) is one of Croatia's best outdoor experiences.

**Brela (60 km)** — Punta Rata beach, repeatedly voted Croatia's most beautiful: fine pebbles, pine shade, and an iconic rock formation in turquoise water. Stop here for 2-3 hours.

**Makarska (90 km)** — your evening base. The Makarska Riviera sits between the Adriatic and the Biokovo mountains (1,762 m) — the backdrop is dramatic. Stroll the palm-lined promenade, eat seafood on the harbour.

**Accommodation:** Makarska or Brela, from €55/night.

![Brela beach Croatia — iconic rock in turquoise water with pine trees](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1592906209472-a36b1f3782ef?w=900&q=80)

### Day 3: Makarska → Biokovo Skywalk → Pelješac Peninsula (120 km)

**Driving:** 2.5 hours with stops

**Biokovo Skywalk (20 km from Makarska)** — glass viewing platform at 1,228 m with 60 km of coastline visible below. Entry: €6 + parking €8. Go early morning — queues form from 10:00.

**Pelješac Peninsula** — Croatia's best-kept secret. Stop at:

**Ston** — the second-longest defensive wall in the world (after the Great Wall of China), 5.5 km across hills. Walk the walls (€12) and then descend for **fresh oysters** directly from the producer — 12 oysters + a glass of Plavac Mali wine: €15. This is genuinely one of the best food experiences in Croatia.

**Dingač wine region** — Croatia's finest red wine. Many cellars offer tastings (€10-15, 3-5 wines).

**Accommodation:** Ston or Orebić, from €50/night.

### Day 4: Pelješac → Trsteno → Dubrovnik (70 km)

**Driving:** 1.5 hours

**Trsteno Arboretum (20 km north of Dubrovnik)** — Renaissance botanical garden (1502) with 500-year-old plane trees and the Neptune fountain — another [Game of Thrones](/en/city-breaks/dubrovnik-city-break-guide) filming location (King's Landing gardens). Entry: €7. A perfect 1-hour stop.

**Dubrovnik — arrival.** Park at Ilijaš garage (near the cable car, €4/hour or €30/day — no cars in the Old Town). Take the cable car up **Mount Srđ** (€27 return) for your first aerial view of the city. The sunset from up here is staggering.

**Accommodation:** Dubrovnik, 2 nights, from €70/night. Stay in Ploče (5 minutes from Old Town, sea views) or Lapad (beach area, 15 minutes by bus).

![Dubrovnik panoramic view from Mount Srđ](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555990793-da11153b2473?w=900&q=80)

### Day 5: Dubrovnik — walls, GoT, island

Full day in Dubrovnik. The suggested schedule:

**8:00** — [City walls](/en/city-breaks/dubrovnik-city-break-guide) (€35). Go at opening — by 10:00 it's packed and brutally hot. The complete circuit: 1.5-2 hours, 1,940 metres of medieval fortification above the sea.

**10:30** — [Game of Thrones walking tour](https://www.getyourguide.com/dubrovnik-l513/game-of-thrones-tours-tc358/?partner_id=EKAA80H) (2 hours, from €25). Filming locations with side-by-side scene comparisons, even if you haven't watched the show.

**13:00** — Lunch at Pizzeria Tabasco (locals' favourite, pizza from €7) or Proto (fine dining, fish, €30/person).

**14:30** — Ferry to **Lokrum Island** (€15 return, 15 minutes). Iron Throne replica (donated by HBO), botanical garden, "Dead Sea" (salt lake for swimming), and rocky beaches with crystal water.

**18:00** — Buža Bar (cliff-edge platform outside the walls). Beer with the best view in Dubrovnik.

## Car rental tips

**Choose full insurance (CDW + TP + Super CDW).** Croatian coastal roads have tight curves and occasional loose gravel. A scratch on the door costs €200-500 without insurance. With Super CDW (€5-10/day extra), excess is zero. [Compare prices on DiscoverCars](https://discovercars.tpo.mx/rR73qao3).

**Photograph the car at pickup.** Every scratch, every side, including roof and wheels. Email the photos to yourself as timestamped proof.

**Diesel or hybrid.** Fuel costs €1.40-1.50/litre. On 350 km with a small diesel: ~25 litres = ~€37.

**One-way fee.** Pick up in Split, drop off in Dubrovnik: €30-80 extra. Worth it — saves a day of driving back and fuel.

**Don't park in Dubrovnik Old Town.** It's pedestrianised. Ilijaš garage (near cable car) or Pile Gate parking (€7/hour) are your options. Both are walking distance from the Old Town gates.
