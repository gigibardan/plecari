If there is one place in Romania where you feel the weight of history, it is Iași.

Not metaphorically. Standing in Palatul Square, you face Romania's largest neo-Gothic building — <cite index="18-1">the Palace of Culture, built between 1906 and 1925 on the site of Stefan the Great's former princely court</cite> — and realise you are standing on the same patch of earth where Alexandru cel Bun, Vasile Lupu, Dimitrie Cantemir and, years later, Mihai Eminescu all passed.

Iași was Moldova's first capital, the place where Alexandru cel Bun strengthened the Moldovan state, where Vasile Lupu built <cite index="18-1">the Three Hierarchs Church with its carved stone ornamentation unrivalled in Romania</cite>, where the Junimea society forged the identity of modern Romanian literature and where Eminescu wrote some of his most fundamental poems.

<cite index="19-1">Today, Iași is a vibrant university centre with 60,000+ students</cite> — a fascinating blend of medieval monuments, neoclassical architecture, academic life and Romania's most important Orthodox spiritual centre.

## Iași at a Glance — Essential Facts

**County:** Iași, northern Moldova.

**Population:** approximately 290,000 — the second largest city in Moldova.

**Historical importance:** Moldova's first medieval capital; centre of modern Romanian culture.

**Spiritual importance:** Cathedral with Saint Parascheva's relics — Romania's most important Orthodox pilgrimage site.

**University:** Alexandru Ioan Cuza University — founded in 1860, Romania's first university.

**Airport:** Iași (IAS) — international, with direct flights from several European cities.

**Average budget:** 120–200 RON/day (~24–40 EUR) — more affordable than Cluj or Brașov.

## How to Get to Iași from Bucharest

### Direct Flight OTP → IAS

Blue Air, Wizz Air operate direct flights. Duration: ~1 hour. 2026 prices: 50–120 EUR return.

**Airport → centre (8 km):**
- **Bolt/Uber**: 20–35 RON (~4–7 EUR), 15 minutes
- **Taxi**: 30–50 RON — verify the meter is running

### By Train

InterCity/InterRegio Bucharest Nord → Iași: ~4–5 hours, 80–130 RON. Comfortable, beautiful scenery through Moldova.

## What to See in Iași

### Palace of Culture — Iași's Symbol

<cite index="18-1">The Palace of Culture is Iași's cultural heart — an impressive neo-Gothic building constructed on the site of the former Princely Court, housing four museums.</cite>

**The 4 museums:**
- **Museum of Art of Moldova** — European paintings (Flemish, Italian, French schools) and Romanian art. Most important art museum in Moldova. Ticket: ~20 RON.
- **Museum of Moldovan History** — history from prehistory to modernity, with rare artefacts. Ticket: ~15 RON.
- **Polytechnic Museum** — history of technology and industry, interactive exhibits. Ticket: ~10 RON.
- **Ethnographic Museum of Moldova** — costumes, objects and traditions of Moldova. Ticket: ~10 RON.

**Combined ticket** (all 4 museums): ~40–50 RON (~8–10 EUR).

Access to the inner courtyard and exterior is free — the Palace is spectacular from every angle.

### Three Hierarchs Church — Unique Stone Ornamentation

<cite index="18-1">The Three Hierarchs Church is a masterpiece of 17th-century Moldovan medieval architecture, renowned for its exterior carved stone ornamentation — a carpet of geometric and floral motifs covering the entire façade, unrivalled in Romania.</cite>

Built by Vasile Lupu in 1639, the church also housed the tombs of Moldovan voivodes. Free entry. Interior with frescoes and a marble iconostasis. Visit duration: 20–30 minutes.

### Metropolitan Cathedral — Saint Parascheva's Relics

<cite index="18-1">The Metropolitan Cathedral is the main cathedral of the Metropolitanate of Moldova, with a richly decorated iconostasis and the relics of Saint Parascheva — patron saint of Moldova.</cite>

On **14 October** (the saint's feast day), over 1 million pilgrims come to Iași — Romania's largest pilgrimage. Free entry.

### Golia Monastery and Tower

<cite index="17-1">Golia Tower is 30 metres high — visitors who want a panoramic view of the city climb 130 steps.</cite> One of Iași's symbols with the best view over the historic centre.

Golia Monastery (17th century) has massive defensive walls and a flower-filled inner courtyard. Free monastery entry; tower: small fee (~5–10 RON).

### Copou Park — Eminescu's Linden Tree

<cite index="19-1">Copou Park is one of Romania's most beautiful parks — with well-maintained alleys, flower beds, ancient trees and cultural monuments.</cite>

**Eminescu's linden tree** — an ancient linden tree under whose shade the poet is said to have rested. Nearby: a small Eminescu museum. Free.

**Lion Obelisk** — the monument built in 1834 to commemorate the Organic Regulation.

**Classic writers' statues** — busts of Romanian classic writers who worked in Iași (Vasile Alecsandri, Mihail Kogălniceanu, Ion Creangă).

### Casa Pogor — Junimea

<cite index="18-1">Casa Pogor is a neoclassical house-museum where the Junimea literary society gathered — the intellectual circle that defined modern Romanian literature in the second half of the 19th century.</cite> Eminescu, Ion Creangă, Ioan Slavici were all members. Ticket: ~10 RON.

## Bukovina Monasteries — The Excursion That Defines Moldova

130–170 km from Iași (1.5–2 hours by car) lie one of Europe's rarest cultural treasures: the **Bukovina Monasteries** with their polychrome exterior frescoes.

<cite index="22-1">Voroneț, Sucevița, Moldovița and Humor Monasteries are UNESCO-inscribed monuments of medieval Moldovan art — exterior frescoes painted in the 15th–16th centuries that have survived 500 years of weather.</cite>

**Voroneț** — "Sistine Chapel of the East" — renowned for Voroneț Blue (unique medieval pigment). Ticket: ~20 RON.

**Sucevița** — best preserved, with frescoes on all four exterior walls. Ticket: ~20 RON.

**Classic circuit from Iași:** morning departure → Voroneț (2 hours) → Sucevița (1.5 hours) → return evening. A full day, ~7–8 hours driving total.

## Food and Drink in Iași

**Moldovan sarmale** — smaller and more delicate than Muntenian versions, wrapped in vine or cabbage leaves, served with sour cream. 30–50 RON at a traditional restaurant.

**Balmoș** — polenta with melted sheep's cheese and sour cream — Moldova's mountain comfort food. 25–35 RON.

**Iași wine — Cotnari Vineyard** — Moldova's most famous wine, produced in Cotnari (50 km from Iași) from native varieties (Grasă de Cotnari, Frâncușă). A glass at a restaurant: 15–25 RON.

**Moldovan cozonac** — fluffy sweet bread with walnuts and raisins, different from the Muntenian version. At any bakery: 30–60 RON.

## Recommended Itinerary — 3 Days in Iași

### Day 1 — Historic Centre

- **Morning:** Palace of Culture (Art Museum + History Museum, 2–3 hours)
- **Lunch:** Moldovan restaurant in the centre with Cotnari wine
- **Afternoon:** Three Hierarchs Church, Metropolitan Cathedral, Golia Monastery + Tower (panorama)
- **Evening:** stroll in the centre, terraces on Unirii Square

### Day 2 — Copou Park and Cultural Life

- **Morning:** Copou Park — Eminescu's linden, Lion Obelisk, classic writers' statues
- **Lunch:** university café
- **Afternoon:** Casa Pogor (Junimea), Ion Creangă Memorial House, Anastasie Fătu Botanic Garden
- **Evening:** performance at the Vasile Alecsandri National Theatre (if in season)

### Day 3 — Bukovina Monasteries (Optional, by Car)

- **Full day:** Voroneț + Sucevița circuit (~7–8 hours return from Iași)
- Or: Cotnari (50 km) — winery visit, authentic Moldovan wine tasting

## Practical Tips

**14 October — Saint Parascheva** — if you want peace, avoid it. If you want Romania's unique pilgrimage experience, go with accommodation booked 3–4 months ahead.

**Bukovina Monasteries** — require a car or guided tour. No convenient public transport.

**Cotnari wine** — buy directly from the Cotnari wineries (50 km from Iași) or from specialist shops in central Iași. Prices 30–40% lower than in Bucharest.

**Climate** — Iași has Romania's most continental climate — hot summers (30–35°C), cold winters (-10 to -15°C possible). Plan your clothing accordingly.
