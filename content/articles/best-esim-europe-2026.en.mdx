## Why you need an eSIM for European travel

If you're from outside the EU, your phone carrier probably charges €5-15 per day for roaming data in Europe. That's €35-105 for a week — just for the privilege of using Google Maps and WhatsApp. An eSIM solves this completely: you buy a data plan for €4-10, install it in 2 minutes, and you have fast local data for your entire trip.

Even if you're an EU resident with free roaming, an eSIM has advantages: guaranteed speed (no fair-use throttling), separate data for work/personal, and coverage in non-EU countries like Turkey, UK (post-Brexit), and the Balkans where roaming doesn't apply.

## How to install an eSIM — step by step

**Before your trip (recommended):**

1. Check your phone supports eSIM (iPhone XS or later, most Samsung/Pixel from 2020+)
2. Buy your plan through the provider's app or website
3. Scan the QR code or install the eSIM profile (Settings > Mobile Data > Add eSIM)
4. Label it "Travel" so you can tell it apart from your regular SIM
5. Keep it disabled until you land — it activates when you turn it on at your destination

**At your destination:**

1. Turn on the eSIM data plan in Settings
2. Set it as your primary data source
3. Keep your regular SIM active for calls/texts (or use WiFi calling)
4. That's it — you're connected

**Total time:** 2-3 minutes. No shop visit, no SIM swapping, no lost contacts.

## Real-world tips

**Buy before you fly.** Some eSIM providers require an internet connection to install the profile. Do it on your home WiFi, not at the airport where WiFi may be spotty.

**Screenshot your QR code.** If anything goes wrong during installation, you'll need the QR code again. Save it to your photos.

**Turn off carrier data roaming.** This prevents your regular SIM from using expensive carrier data. Your eSIM handles all data; your regular SIM handles calls/texts only.

**Check hotspot/tethering.** If you're travelling with someone who doesn't have eSIM support, check that your plan allows hotspot sharing. Most do, but some budget plans restrict it.

**Don't delete the eSIM profile.** If you remove the profile, you may not be able to reinstall it. Just turn it off when you don't need it.
