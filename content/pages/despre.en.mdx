## Hi, I'm Radu

I built **Plecări** out of a simple frustration: every time I planned a trip, I'd end up with 30 tabs open — one for flights, another for hotels, another for insurance, another for eSIM, another for things to do at the destination. Each tab in a different language, with different formats and different gotchas.

Plecări is the answer to that chaos: **one place to compare, understand, and book** — from your flight to the experiences waiting at your destination — with information written by someone who has actually been through the process.

## My background in travel

I'm not a travel blogger who posts sunset photos from Santorini. I come from **the cruise industry**, where I spent several years on the technical side — integrating with booking systems, mapping itineraries, configuring cabin layouts and pricing. I know how a booking engine works from the inside, not just from the interface a tourist sees.

That experience allows me to write cruise guides with details you won't find in typical reviews: how cruise lines actually compare, which cabin to choose for your real needs, and how to avoid hidden costs.

Beyond cruises, I've built and managed online travel platforms, which means I understand both the traveller's perspective and the mechanics behind the deals you see.

## How Plecări works

Plecări covers **seven travel verticals**: flights, stays, insurance, cruises, eSIM and roaming, car rental, and city breaks, plus experiences and activities at your destination.

Each article combines editorial content (guides, comparisons, practical tips) with **search and comparison tools** connected to real booking platforms. When you find something that fits and book it, the partner platform pays us a commission — at no extra cost to you.

That's the entire model: we write useful content, connect it to trusted partners, and only get paid if we've actually helped you. For full details, see our [affiliate disclosure](/en/legal/disclosure).

## What sets us apart

- **Real industry experience**, not just tourist opinions
- **Bilingual content** (Romanian and English), written by a native speaker
- **Integrated search tools**, not just links — you can actually compare prices and options
- **Full transparency** — every page clearly states that some links are affiliate links
