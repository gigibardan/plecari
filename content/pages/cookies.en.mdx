This page explains what cookies and similar technologies Plecări.com uses, why each one exists, and how you can control what you accept.

---

## What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that your browser saves on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember certain information between visits — for example, that you've already accepted the cookie banner and don't need to see it again.

Not all cookies are the same. Some are strictly necessary for the site to function; others are optional and only activate with your explicit consent.

---

## What cookies we use and why

### Essential cookies — no consent required

These cookies are necessary for the basic functioning of the site and cannot be disabled.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Cookie preference | Remembers whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies | 12 months |
| Language preference | Remembers whether you chose Romanian or English | Session |

**They transmit no identifiable data and do not track your behaviour.**

---

### Analytics cookies — with your consent

I use a web analytics tool to understand how the site is used. These cookies collect **aggregated and anonymised** data — they don't know who you are, only that someone visited a particular page.

**What they measure:**
- How many visits each page receives
- Where traffic comes from (Google, social media, direct)
- What devices and browsers visitors use
- How long users spend on the site

**What they don't do:**
- They don't build individual profiles
- They don't track your behaviour outside this site
- They don't transmit identifiable data anywhere

If you don't accept this category, the site works exactly the same — I simply won't have traffic data.

---

### Affiliate cookies — with your consent

Plecări.com promotes travel services through affiliate links (Booking.com, Airalo, etc.). When you click such a link, the partner places a cookie that lets them know the booking came from our site — which is how we earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

**What this means in practice:**
- The partner knows you came from Plecări.com
- I don't know who you are or what you booked
- The commission is calculated automatically based on the attribution cookie

**These cookies are managed by third-party partners** (Booking.com, affiliate networks), not by me directly. Their privacy policies govern how they handle that data.

If you don't accept this category, booking links continue to work normally — I simply can't receive a commission if you make a booking.

## Third-party cookies

Search and comparison widgets (for example, the flight or hotel search engine) may load cookies from those platforms. These activate **only after you accept** the corresponding category.

The main third parties whose cookies may appear:

- **Booking.com** — for accommodation search widgets
- **Affiliate networks** — for booking attribution

Each has its own cookie policy, which you can read on their websites.

## How to manage your preferences

**On your first visit**, a banner appears where you can accept or reject each category of optional cookies.

**Later**, you can change your choices at any time in two ways:

1. **Through your browser settings** — you can delete existing cookies or block new ones. Deleting the preference cookie will bring back the consent banner on your next visit.

2. **Through per-site browser settings** — all modern browsers allow per-site management.

**Quick guides for the main browsers:**
- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
- Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- Safari: Preferences → Privacy
- Edge: Settings → Privacy, search and services → Cookies

## What we don't use

To be completely transparent — there is **no** such thing on Plecări.com:

- Behavioural advertising cookies (Facebook Pixel, Google Ads remarketing)
- Cross-site tracking cookies
- Profiling or audience segmentation cookies
- Any form of fingerprinting
