This page explains, in plain language and without unnecessary legal jargon, how Plecări.com handles your data. The short version: **I don't intentionally collect personal data, I don't sell it, I don't use it for marketing, and I don't share it with anyone beyond the technical tools that keep this site running.**

This policy complies with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

---

## Who runs this site

Plecări.com is an independent site run by one person. There is no company behind it, no team. Just one person writing travel guides.

Contact: **contact@plecari.com**

---

## What data I collect and why

### 1. Traffic analytics

I use a web analytics tool to understand how many people visit the site, which pages are popular, where visitors come from, and what devices they use. That's it.

**What this means in practice:**
- Number of visits and unique visitors
- Pages viewed and session duration
- Traffic source (Google, direct, social media)
- Device type (desktop/mobile/tablet) and browser
- Country of origin (not your exact location)

**What it doesn't mean:**
- I don't know who you are
- I don't see your IP address in an identifiable form
- I don't build a profile of you
- I don't track what you do outside this site

This data is **aggregated and anonymised** — I can see that "200 people read the Egypt guide in November", not "John Smith from London read it on Tuesday at 2:32pm".

### 2. Contact data

If you send me an email at `contact@plecari.com`, I receive your email address and the content of your message. I use it solely to reply to you. I don't store it in any separate database, don't add you to any mailing list, and don't share it with anyone.

### 3. Affiliate cookies

When you click a booking link (Booking.com, Airalo, etc.) and make a booking, the partner places a cookie to attribute the commission correctly. This doesn't transmit your personal data to me — the partner knows a booking came from Plecări.com, but I don't know who you are. Full details in the [Cookie Policy](/en/legal/cookies).

## What I don't do with your data

To be completely clear:

- **I don't sell data** — no visitor data goes to third parties for commercial purposes
- **I don't do email marketing** — there is no newsletter, no subscriber list
- **I don't create profiles** — I don't track individual visitor behaviour
- **I don't use behavioural advertising** — there is no Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, or targeted advertising on this site
- **I don't store data longer than necessary** — emails received are not systematically archived

| Service | Role | Where it processes |
|---|---|---|

## How long data is kept

**Analytics data** is aggregated and doesn't have a clear individual "expiry" — it's statistics, not individual records. Raw data (if any) follows the retention policy of the tool used.

**Emails** are not systematically archived. If you write to me and I reply, the conversation stays in my email inbox like any other email.

**Cookies** — see the [Cookie Policy](/en/legal/cookies) for details.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Even though I don't actively collect personal data, you have rights guaranteed by GDPR:

- **Access** — you can ask what data I hold about you
- **Rectification** — you can ask for inaccurate data to be corrected
- **Erasure** — you can ask for your data to be deleted
- **Restriction** — you can ask for processing to be limited
- **Objection** — you can object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Portability** — you can request your data in a structured format

For any such request, write to **contact@plecari.com**. I'll aim to respond within 30 days.

If you believe your rights haven't been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country. In Romania, that's **ANSPDCP** — [dataprotection.ro](https://www.dataprotection.ro). In the EU, you can find your national authority at [edpb.europa.eu](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en).
