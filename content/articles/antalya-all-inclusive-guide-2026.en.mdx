## Why Antalya dominates the all-inclusive market

Antalya's formula is hard to beat: massive resort hotels with multiple pools, waterparks, private beaches, à la carte restaurants, kids' clubs, evening entertainment — all included in a single price. The Turkish Riviera has perfected the all-inclusive model to a degree that European equivalents (Spain, Greece) simply can't match at the same price point.

A week's all-inclusive with flights starts at €330/person. For that money in Spain or Italy, you'd barely cover the hotel without meals.

## The four zones — which is right for you

### Lara — "The Las Vegas of the Turkish Riviera"

15 minutes from the airport. Lara has the largest, most spectacular themed hotels — complexes designed to look like palaces, with enormous aquaparks, multiple swimming pools, and entertainment that runs from morning yoga to midnight shows.

**Best for:** families with kids (aquaparks are extraordinary), first-time Turkey visitors, anyone who wants to be impressed by scale.

**Beach:** fine golden sand, gentle entry into the water — ideal for small children.

**Packages:** 7 nights 4-5★ AI with flights from €400-800/person.

### Belek — luxury and golf

30-40 minutes from the airport. Belek is the premium zone: quieter, more manicured, with championship golf courses and high-end spa facilities. The hotels are newer and the atmosphere more refined.

**Best for:** couples, golfers, those who want luxury at Turkish prices, anniversary trips.

**Packages:** 7 nights 5★ AI from €600-1,200/person.

### Side — history meets beach

60-75 minutes from the airport. Side has something no other Turkish resort has: a Roman amphitheatre and the Temple of Apollo **on the beach**. Ancient ruins literally metres from your sun lounger. The town itself has character — cobblestone streets, a harbour, and local restaurants that aren't hotel-chain generic.

**Best for:** families with older children, travellers who want culture alongside the beach, mid-range budgets.

**Packages:** 7 nights 4★ AI from €350-600/person.

Book [Side excursions on GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/side-l2576/?partner_id=EKAA80H) — the Manavgat Waterfall and ancient Aspendos are excellent half-day trips.

### Kemer — mountains meet sea

45-60 minutes from the airport. Kemer has the most dramatic scenery: the Taurus Mountains plunge directly into the sea, creating small turquoise bays. Beaches are pebbly (not sand), which makes the water impossibly clear.

**Best for:** active couples, nature lovers, divers (30m+ visibility), those who prefer natural beauty over hotel spectacle.

**Packages:** 7 nights 4★ AI from €330-550/person — the most affordable zone.

## All-inclusive vs. ultra all-inclusive — what's the difference

| Feature | Standard AI | Ultra AI |
|---|---|---|
| Buffet meals (3/day) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Local drinks (beer, wine, spirits) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Imported spirits (Johnnie Walker, etc.) | ❌ | ✅ |
| À la carte restaurants | 1-2 per stay (reservation) | Unlimited, no reservation |
| 24/7 snacks | Limited hours | Round the clock |
| Minibar | Restocked on request | Restocked daily |
| Ice cream | At designated times | Anytime |
| Room service | ❌ (most hotels) | ✅ |

**The verdict:** if you drink imported spirits or want unlimited à la carte dining (Italian, Asian, seafood, steakhouse), ultra AI pays for itself. The upgrade typically costs €50-100/person for the entire week.

## Excursions worth leaving the hotel for

**Pamukkale** (3-4 hours from Antalya, day trip €50-70) — white travertine terraces with natural thermal pools, plus the ancient ruins of Hierapolis. One of Turkey's most photographed sites. [Book on GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/antalya-l207/?partner_id=EKAA80H) for transport + guide.

**Kaleiçi Old Town** (free, 20-30 minutes from Lara by bus) — Antalya's historic centre with Hadrian's Gate (129 AD), the old harbour, and the bazaar. A perfect half-day away from the hotel.

**Düden Waterfalls** (€10-25 excursion) — 40m waterfall dropping directly into the sea. Especially impressive from a boat tour.

**Boat trip along the coast** (€20-35, full day) — sailing along the coastline with swimming stops in secluded bays. Lunch on board included. Available from all zones.

**Rafting on Köprülü Canyon** (€40-50, full day) — spectacular turquoise canyon, Grade 1-2 rapids (accessible for families with kids 10+). One of Turkey's best outdoor experiences.

## Practical tips

**Passport required.** Turkey is not in the EU. Most nationalities: visa-free for 90 days, but passport must be valid 6 months from entry date. No national ID cards accepted.

**Currency:** Turkish Lira (TRY). €1 ≈ 36-38 TRY. Cards accepted at hotels and larger shops. Cash useful for bazaars and local restaurants (negotiate in EUR or USD, but pay in TRY for best rates).

**Bargaining at bazaars:** expected and part of the culture. Start at 50% of the asking price and settle at 60-70%.

**[Travel insurance](https://ektatraveling.tpo.mx/uU7jZ5jK) is essential.** EHIC/GHIC does NOT work in Turkey. A night in a private Turkish hospital costs €500-2,000. Insurance costs €15-25/week. [Full guide](/en/asigurari/travel-insurance-europe-guide).

**[eSIM](https://airalo.tpo.mx/mYekFAo3) instead of roaming.** Turkey is NOT in the EU — your carrier charges €3-15/day for roaming. An eSIM costs €4-5 for 1GB/7 days. [Comparison guide](/en/esim-roaming/best-esim-europe-2026).

**Best months:** May-June and September-October. Pleasant 25-32°C, cheaper packages, pools are quiet. July-August: 38-42°C, everything is packed, and excursions become exhausting.
