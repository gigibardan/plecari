We want to be transparent about how this site is supported.

## What "affiliate link" means

Plecări contains affiliate links and search tools. If you access some offers through them and make a booking or purchase, we may receive a commission from the partner. **The price you pay stays the same** — the commission is covered by the partner, not by you.

## Who we work with

We collaborate with travel networks and brands through affiliate platforms (for example Travelpayouts and its partners), covering flights, stays, insurance, eSIM, car rental and more. The list of partners may change over time.

## Our commitment

Commissions do not influence the honesty of our recommendations. We present information as accurately and usefully as possible, and booking decisions are entirely yours. We do not guarantee prices or availability — these are set by the partners and may change.

## Cookies

Affiliate links and widgets may set cookies, which load only with your consent. See the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).
