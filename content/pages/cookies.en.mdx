This site uses cookies and similar technologies. Below we explain what they are, which types we use, and how you can control your choices.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored by your browser on your device. They help the site work, help us understand how it is used, and — with your consent — correctly attribute bookings made through our partners.

## Cookie categories

- **Essential** — required for the site's basic functionality (e.g. remembering your cookie preference). No consent needed.
- **Analytics** — show us, in aggregate, how the site is used, so we can improve it. Enabled only with your consent.
- **Affiliate / marketing** — come from the partners through which we promote travel services (affiliate networks and widgets). They allow a booking to be attributed to us, at no extra cost to you. Enabled only with your consent.

## Third-party cookies

The search and comparison tools (widgets) and affiliate links may set cookies managed by those partners. These load **only after you accept** the corresponding category.

## How to manage your choices

On your first visit, we ask for your consent through a banner. You can withdraw or change your choice at any time by deleting cookies in your browser settings, which will bring the consent banner back. You can also block or delete cookies directly in your browser.
