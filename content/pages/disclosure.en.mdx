Plecări.com contains affiliate links. This page explains exactly what that means, how it works, and how it does (or doesn't) affect the content I write.

---

## What is an affiliate link

An affiliate link is a tracking link that tells a partner that your visit or booking came from Plecări.com. When you:

1. Click such a link
2. Visit the partner's website
3. Make a booking or purchase (sometimes within a 24–72 hour window)

...the partner pays me a small commission. **You don't pay more** — the price is the same as if you had gone directly to the partner's site. The commission comes from the merchant's margin, not your pocket.

---

## How it works technically

When you click an affiliate link, your browser saves a tracking cookie on your device. If you make a booking within the attribution window (usually 24–72 hours, varies per partner), the partner identifies that the booking came from Plecări.com and calculates the commission.

If you don't agree with this type of tracking, you can:
- Decline the "Affiliate cookies" category on the consent banner
- Visit partner sites directly, without going through Plecări.com links

---

## Commissions don't influence content

**This is my commitment to you:**

- I don't recommend services I wouldn't consider useful, regardless of the commission
- I don't hide better options because they don't generate commission
- I don't adjust ratings or conclusions based on partnerships
- When a service has both advantages and disadvantages, I write about both

Affiliate commissions are how Plecări.com supports itself financially without traditional advertising. Without them, the site wouldn't exist. But I'm not willing to compromise the usefulness of the content for more monetised traffic — that would eliminate the very reason someone would come back to the site.

---

## Prices and availability

Prices, availability and offer conditions shown or mentioned in articles are set by partners and may change without notice. I try to update information as often as possible, but I can't guarantee data is always accurate in real time. Always check the final price on the partner's site before booking.
