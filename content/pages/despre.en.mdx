## Hi, I'm Gigi

I built **Plecări.com** out of a frustration I'm pretty sure you know too: every time I planned a trip, I'd end up with 30 tabs open — one for flights, another for hotels, another for insurance, another for eSIM, another for things to do at the destination. Each tab in a different language, with different formats and different traps.

And after hours of research, I still didn't feel like I had the full picture.

**Plecări.com** is the answer to that chaos: one place to compare, understand, and book — from your flight to the experiences waiting at your destination — with information written by someone who has actually been through the same process.

---

## Where the passion for travel came from

I didn't discover travel through books. I discovered it by **making mistakes** — a flight booked at the wrong time and paying twice the price, accommodation that looked exactly like the photos but wasn't, a bank card blocked abroad, an eSIM bought at the airport for three times the going rate.

Every mistake taught me something. And at some point, I realized I knew enough to save other people from making the same ones.

That's what Plecări.com really is: **lessons from years of travelling**, turned into practical guides I wish I'd had before my own first mistakes.

## How Plecări.com works

Plecări covers **seven travel verticals**: flights, accommodation, insurance, cruises, eSIM and roaming, car rental, and city breaks, plus experiences and activities at your destination.

Each article combines:
- **Practical guides** written after real research, not press releases
- **Honest comparisons** that include the downsides, not just the upsides
- **Integrated search tools** connected to real booking platforms

When you find something that fits and book it, the partner platform pays us a commission — **at no extra cost to you**. For full details, see our [affiliate disclosure](/en/legal/disclosure).

The model is simple: we write genuinely useful content, connect it to trusted partners, and only get paid if we've actually helped.

## What sets us apart

There are thousands of travel sites. Here's why Plecări.com is different:

**We write for real travelers.** Not translated content. Original guides written from actual experience — what it's like to travel from Romania, with a Romanian passport, using a Revolut card, wondering whether you need a visa and how to avoid bank fees.

**Prices are real.** We don't write "prices from X EUR" without context. We give prices with dates, with season, with what's included and what isn't.

**We say what doesn't work too.** If a service has important downsides, we write them. If a destination has safety concerns, we mention them. Transparency isn't optional.

**Bilingual content.** Every important guide exists in both Romanian and English, because we travel with international friends and want to understand sources in both languages.
