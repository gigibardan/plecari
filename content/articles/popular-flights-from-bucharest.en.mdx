## Wizz Air, Ryanair, TAROM and Turkish: real comparison

### Wizz Air: the Bucharest market leader

Wizz Air operates from Bucharest Otopeni (OTP) and has the largest direct route network from Romania — 60+ destinations. Its fundamental advantage: low base fares and dense coverage across Europe.

**Strengths:** 60+ direct destinations, competitive base fares, excellent app, WIZZ Priority adds good value.

**Weaknesses:** hold baggage always extra (€20–45/segment), seats not allocated without additional payment, changes are costly.

**Real cost calculation for Wizz Air:**
- Base fare (ticket + small hand luggage 40×30×20 cm): **€X**
- + WIZZ Priority (cabin bag in overhead + priority boarding): **+€6–15/segment**
- + Hold baggage 20 kg: **+€20–45/segment**
- + Seat selection: **+€5–15/segment**

A ticket displayed at €45 return with hold baggage and a seat = €115–155 total. Always calculate the complete price.

### Ryanair: fast expansion at OTP

Ryanair has significantly grown its Bucharest presence from 2023–2025, now operating 35+ direct routes. It is frequently cheaper than Wizz Air on Rome Ciampino, Milan Bergamo, and London Stansted/Luton.

**Watch out for:** Ryanair typically uses secondary airports. Add the transfer cost before comparing:

| Route | Ryanair airport | Transfer to centre | Cost | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rome | Ciampino (CIA) | Bus → Termini | €6 | 45 min |
| Milan | Bergamo (BGY) | Bus → Central | €7 | 60 min |
| London | Stansted (STN) | Stansted Express train | £25 | 50 min |
| London | Luton (LTN) | Bus + train | £18 | 60 min |
| Paris | Beauvais (BVA) | Bus → Porte Maillot | €17 | 75 min |

If Ryanair base fare + transfer > Wizz Air to the main airport, Wizz Air wins on total cost.

### TAROM: when it makes sense

TAROM is frequently overlooked because the displayed fare appears higher. But TAROM includes checked baggage (23 kg) and an in-flight meal — the total price compared with Wizz Air + baggage is often similar or in TAROM's favour.

**TAROM wins when:**
- You need flexibility (some fare classes allow free changes)
- You have hold baggage and the total cost comparison is close
- Connecting through major hubs (Frankfurt via Lufthansa codeshare)
- You value comfort over the absolute lowest price

### Turkish Airlines: gateway to the world

For any destination outside Europe, Turkish Airlines via Istanbul (IST or SAW) is typically the most competitive option from Bucharest. The Istanbul hub has connections to virtually every country on earth, multiple daily Bucharest connections, and competitive pricing.

**Typical round-trip prices from Bucharest via Turkish Airlines:**
- New York (JFK): €600–950
- Dubai (DXB): €350–600
- Bangkok (BKK): €500–800
- Tokyo (NRT): €700–1,100
- Johannesburg (JNB): €600–900
- Mumbai (BOM): €450–700

## How to compare correctly: total price, not base fare

The most common mistake: comparing a €40 Wizz Air base fare with a €90 TAROM fare and choosing Wizz. But Wizz with baggage + seat = €90–130; TAROM with baggage included = €90. They are identical or TAROM is cheaper.

**The correct formula:**

**Total cost = Ticket fare + Hold baggage (if needed) + Seat selection (if travelling together) + Secondary airport transfer (if applicable)**

Always use a search engine that shows all airlines simultaneously — Kiwi.com or Google Flights. Never search only on the airline's own website, as airlines do not show each other's prices.

## Connection flights from Bucharest: when they make sense

### When connections are unavoidable

All intercontinental destinations require a connection from Bucharest — no direct long-haul flights exist from OTP.

### Best hub connections from Bucharest

**Istanbul (Turkish Airlines):** most widely used. Multiple daily flights, huge global network, competitive prices. The 1–2 hour connection is typically smooth at IST (well-designed transit zone).

**Frankfurt (Lufthansa):** premium hub for the USA and Asia. Higher quality connections, slightly higher fares.

**Amsterdam (KLM):** excellent for North America (New York, Boston, Toronto, Vancouver).

**Vienna (Austrian Airlines):** close to Bucharest (1h40), frequent, good option for destinations where Austrian has direct services.

### Open-jaw routing: the experienced traveller's trick

Fly outbound to Rome and return from Barcelona. This lets you explore two cities without retracing your steps — and costs approximately the same as a standard return on one route. Search engines that support open-jaw routing (Kiwi.com does this well) automatically find these combinations.

## Your rights on flights from Bucharest

### EU261 compensation

All flights **departing from Bucharest** are covered by EU Regulation 261/2004, regardless of which airline operates the flight. If your flight is delayed by 3+ hours at your final destination or cancelled with less than 14 days' notice:

| Route distance | Compensation |
|---|---|
| Under 1,500 km (Budapest, Rome, Istanbul, Athens) | **€250** |
| 1,500–3,500 km (London, Amsterdam, Madrid, Paris) | **€400** |
| Over 3,500 km | **€600** |

You can claim for past flights up to 3 years after the disruption. [AirHelp](https://airhelp.tpo.mx/Etx51IpP) and [Compensair](https://compensair.tpo.mx/wi3KeRBP) process claims on a success-fee basis.

Full guide: [flight delay and cancellation rights EU261](/en/zboruri/flight-delay-passenger-rights).
